# Cowrie — Measured Gains, Use Cases & Engineering Playbook (`v1.2.0`)

> **Core is physics, profiles are chemistry, lenses are optics.**

Cowrie (`v1.2.0`) is a **16 KB-aligned, content-addressed, trust-tagged binary container** designed for the tri-consumer collision of AI systems: an **LLM attention engine** (paying per token), a **GPU/NPU arithmetic engine** (requiring page-aligned zero-copy `mmap` and `O_DIRECT` DMA), and a **CPU security/cache verifier** (requiring zero-allocation bijective hashing, unforgeable origin tags, and `Ed25519` auditability).
