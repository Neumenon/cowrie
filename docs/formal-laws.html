Chapter 01 · Independent Dual Verification

Dual-Prover Architecture & Honest Trust Boundary

0 External Deps · 100% Constructive

A single formal suite can hide two subtle failure modes: a quirk in one proof assistant’s kernel or encoding, or a circular specification that merely restates its own implementation. Cowrie eliminates both by maintaining two completely independent formalizations in distinct type theories—Bend 2.0.35 (checked by bend2 and the Lean-proven BendTT kernel) and Lean 4.34.0 (pure Init, zero external Lake packages, re-checked from scratch by standalone leanchecker --fresh)—with frozen specification modules strictly isolated from mutable kernels.

Zero-Classical Constructive Discipline

Every one of the 40 Lean 4 laws and 16 constructive semantic refutations—including the bit-exact 64-bit Int64 ↔ UInt64 ZigZag bijection (L37), fuel-free universal RFC 6962 Merkle injectivity (L38), and declarative List.Infix prefix safety (L39)—checks strictly under [propext, Quot.sound]. Not even Classical.choice is used, and zero sorry, admit, axiom, native_decide, bv_decide, unsafe, or extern constructs exist anywhere in the proof tree.

Why Clean-Room TCB Isolation Matters In Bend, all 111 frozen spec predicates live in formal/SPEC.bend while mutable implementations live in CowrieKernel.bend. In Lean, all frozen specs live in Cowrie/Laws.lean and are type-bound via Cowrie.AxiomAudit._bindCowrieSpec : Cowrie.Laws.CowrieSpec. Mutating a kernel can never silently move the goalposts.

Chapter 03 · Adversarial Proof Auditing

Two-Round Multi-Agent Critique Ledger

5 Independent Audit Agents

Formal proofs are only as strong as the theorems they state. Before and after writing the Lean 4 mirror and upgrading the Bend suite from 26 to 37 laws, five specialized read-only research subagents adversarially attacked both the Rust implementation and the formal law statements themselves—actively searching for vacuous implications, circular self-checks, TCB leaks, missing boundary branches, and harness gaps.

Every Finding Closed Zero findings were hand-waved or deferred. Each formal weakness gained a dedicated regression mutant (M14–M34, LM06–LM26) or constructive refutation (R1–R16); each Rust/oracle weakness became a verified fix (F9–F18).
